Frequently asked

How participation works.

Can an independent broadcaster join?

Yes. RMA is designed to connect independent media voices as well as established networks.

Does RMA own partner channels?

No. Partner organisations keep their own identity and operations while collaborating through the association.

How do I get involved?

Contact Alex Mishra at tagnz@cirya.co with your channel, idea, or event proposal.
